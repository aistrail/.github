AIS Trail collects AIS messages from licensed public sources and a network of volunteer receiving stations, cleans them
into one schema and serves them as vessel positions, tracks, port calls and vessel events.

| Product | What it does |
|---|---|
| [REST API](https://aistrail.com/docs/) | Vessel search and details, tracks, area queries, port calls and events, in JSON, GeoJSON or CSV; live positions and a WebSocket stream on Pro and Business |
| [MCP server](https://github.com/aistrail/mcp) | The same data inside Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and other MCP clients, with sanctions list flags |
| [Live map](https://map.aistrail.com) | Ships in real time, free and without an account; vessel and port pages, coverage map |
| [Receiver network](https://aistrail.com/stations/) | Share the data of your own AIS receiver and appear on the public station list |

Plans are credit-based and include a Free plan. Coverage and data sources: [coverage map](https://map.aistrail.com/coverage)
and [sources](https://aistrail.com/docs/sources/). Not for navigation.
